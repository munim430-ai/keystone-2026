📌 December Intake Availability Summary

EAP & KLP Programs: DECEMBER INTAKE OPEN — The Winter term for English (EAP) & Korean (KLP) language preparatory programs starts in December. Applications are accepted through October–November.

Bachelor's Degree Programs: APPLICATION WINDOW OPEN — Degree classes start in March (Spring) and September (Fall). While degree classes do not start in December, December is the peak application deadline window for the Spring (March) intake.

2. Admission & Entry Requirements

  • Academic Background: High School Diploma / HSC / A-Levels equivalent with minimum GPA 3.0/5.0.
  • Study Gap: Maximum 3 years gap from last formal graduation date.
  • Language Proficiency:
    • EAP Pathway: Minimum IELTS 5.0.
    • Bachelor Direct Entry: Minimum IELTS 5.5 / 6.0 or TOEFL iBT 71.
    • Korean Track: Minimum TOPIK Level 3.
  • Financial Capacity: Bank statement & certificate showing minimum USD $16,000 (6-month history, max 30 days old at document lock).
